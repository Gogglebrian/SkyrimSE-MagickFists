SkyrimSE+SKSE mod: Adds six new enchantments for gauntlets, which confer 
their respective effects to unarmed strikes: Fire, Frost, Shock,
Absorb Health, Paralysis, and Chaos.

All can be disenchanted and used in custom enchantments, and will be
affected by relevant Enchanting perks and Augmented element perks from 
the Destruction tree.

These enchantments only work for the player. 

Five of the effects (all besides Chaos) can be obtained from a chest near
Gian the Fist in the Ratway in Riften. The Chaos enchantment can be found
in a chest near General Carius in Fort Frostmoth.

Compatibility: doesn't touch any vanilla content except for adding the 
chests to the Ratway and Fort Frostmoth.
